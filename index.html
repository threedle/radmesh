RADmesh: Remesh-Aware Mesh Deformation

1University of Chicago, 2University of Southern California, 3Technion
Summary BibTeX

RADmesh deforms and remeshes a mesh, or a region of a mesh, towards a text prompt. As a generative deformation technique enhanced by remeshing, RADmesh is capable of growing large, detailed geometry that extends far from the bounds of the original mesh, with sharp detail even at extremities. Our coarse-to-fine remeshing schedule and optimizer state interpolation help us achieve superior visual quality while keeping triangles neat and efficient.
